# Thingtesting research

Reviewed public profiles in the browser on 4 November 2026. The text-fetching web tool returned access errors; the normal browser rendered the public profiles successfully.

The atlas uses Thingtesting for discovery and a short qualitative community lens. It does not publish ratings as comparative evidence, infer reviewer ages, or endorse efficacy/safety claims. indu had no visible reviews; Dabble & Dollop and Sincerely Yours had especially small samples. Namesake clothing brands were excluded when identifying Sincerely Yours skincare.

Verified profiles and original observations are in `dist/thingtesting.js`. The public Baby & Kids category is the discovery entry point: https://thingtesting.com/categories/baby-and-kids.

Raw browser reading notes remain local and are ignored by Git. They are not part of the published site or source handoff.
