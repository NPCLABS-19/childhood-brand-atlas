# Small Worlds

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
The user wants a rich comparative reading of children's lifestyle, cosmetic, personal-care and skin-protection brands, with a separate teenage section, and a place to work with creative-assignment responses. Assumed use: creative and brand strategy research. Repository collaborators can edit and publish a shared Bento board; visitors can read the published result.

## Product Purpose
Make perception, target group, voice, child versus parent address, visual identity, archetype, illustration, core product and category breadth easy to understand and compare. Preserve submitted creative work with its questions, expose useful colour and pattern extracts, and let the studio assemble and share a nine-tile visual board.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Retain the eight original lifestyle profiles and expand with children’s care, cosmetics and sun-protection brands, plus six separate teen profiles. Browsable profiles, segment-specific positioning maps, cross-brand comparison and sources. Use Thingtesting for discovery and qualitative community context, alongside official sources for category facts. Psychographic target groups, archetypes and perceptual positions are editorial interpretation, not customer research. This is brand analysis, not product-use or clinical guidance.

Add WIP to the visible main navigation, then a contributor level and Responses / Colours / Patterns. The first contributor is Amaira: one submission dated 17 November 2026, all 15 questions, 13 image attachments, question 11 ranked Adidas / Oreo / Disney, and question 13 unanswered. Preserve the original prompts, filenames, source links and unanswered state. The import is a captured submission, not a live Forms synchronization. Eight artwork colours and five direct crops from the question 9 shape study are working references with recorded extraction methods.

Add Bentos as a main destination. Match the Theaterbaren reference’s exact nine-tile geometry, proportions, spacing and corners. Each tile supports image, GIF or video upload, complete-image/fill choice, crop position and background colour. Include preview, motion pause, a 2160 px square image download, portable project download/import and browser draft persistence. A draft stays local until a repository writer chooses Publish to everyone. The shared board and media are versioned in the same repository as the site; publishing must preserve concurrent source changes, refuse to overwrite a newer board and distinguish a saved GitHub commit from a live Pages build.

## Delivery Constraints
Publish only to the user’s `NPCLABS-19/childhood-brand-atlas` repository and GitHub Pages. Source lives on `main`; Pages serves the contents of `dist` from `gh-pages`. Keep Pages history by creating a new commit with the current Pages head as parent; never force-push a detached replacement history. The in-page publisher uses a fine-grained repository token with Contents and Pages read/write access, kept in page memory and sent only to GitHub’s API. Local visual review and live deployment verification are separate completion checks.

## Brand Commitments
Clean, legible, restrained design. Sans-serif only. Visible navigation, no hamburger menus. Real imagery, minimal copy per view, no redundant text or decorative overdesign. The user explicitly clarified that 19-studio.com and npclabs-19.github.io/chocolate-studio/ are references for the presentation's own aesthetic, not ends of a brand spectrum. Use their neutral grey ground, tight sans-serif typography and precise, visible controls.

WIP and Bentos inherit the existing system rather than replacing it. The white Bento canvas and rounded tile corners reproduce the explicit Theaterbaren reference; that exception belongs to the board composition and does not establish a site-wide card system. Extracted artwork colours remain content, not interface theme colours.

## Evidence on Hand
Official brand about pages and current category navigation; sourced imagery with origin records. No consumer interviews, sales data, demographic surveys or verified market shares.

The creative-assignment data retains the source form and attachment URLs, with separate provenance for downloaded and losslessly re-encoded PNG files. Colour sampling regions, pattern crop coordinates and the Bento reference geometry are recorded under `research/`. These extracts describe the supplied artwork; they are not a measured brand palette or new survey findings.

## Product Principles
Show visual evidence alongside interpretation. Separate user, buyer and communication audience. Use qualitative placements instead of fabricated statistics. Put depth inside profiles rather than crowding the overview.

Keep submitted evidence distinct from the studio’s extracts and board composition. Preserve missing answers instead of completing them by inference. Make draft, saved and published states clear, and keep a portable media-complete project available alongside browser persistence.
