# Small Worlds

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
The user wants a rich comparative reading of children's lifestyle, cosmetic, personal-care and skin-protection brands, with a separate teenage section. Assumed use: creative and brand strategy research.

## Product Purpose
Make perception, target group, voice, child versus parent address, visual identity, archetype, illustration, core product and category breadth easy to understand and compare.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Retain the eight original lifestyle profiles and expand with children’s care, cosmetics and sun-protection brands, plus six separate teen profiles. Browsable profiles, segment-specific positioning maps, cross-brand comparison and sources. Use Thingtesting for discovery and qualitative community context, alongside official sources for category facts. Psychographic target groups, archetypes and perceptual positions are editorial interpretation, not customer research. This is brand analysis, not product-use or clinical guidance.

## Delivery Constraints
This expansion was previewed locally and the user approved deployment in the follow-up instruction “deploy it”. Publish to NPCLABS-19 GitHub Pages only.

## Brand Commitments
Clean, legible, restrained design. Sans-serif only. Visible navigation, no hamburger menus. Real imagery, minimal copy per view, no redundant text or decorative overdesign. The user explicitly clarified that 19-studio.com and npclabs-19.github.io/chocolate-studio/ are references for the presentation's own aesthetic, not ends of a brand spectrum. Use their neutral grey ground, tight sans-serif typography and precise, visible controls.

## Evidence on Hand
Official brand about pages and current category navigation; sourced imagery with origin records. No consumer interviews, sales data, demographic surveys or verified market shares.

## Product Principles
Show visual evidence alongside interpretation. Separate user, buyer and communication audience. Use qualitative placements instead of fabricated statistics. Put depth inside profiles rather than crowding the overview.
